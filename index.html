01 — Research

Clinical models that keep their assumptions visible.

I am an undergraduate researcher and software engineer at BRAC University, finishing a B.Sc. in Computer Science and Engineering in September 2026 and looking for research opportunities in trustworthy, human-centered AI. The work applies deep learning to clinical diagnostics, biomedical image analysis, and distributed healthcare: federated learning when hospital records cannot move, multimodal late fusion for early prediction, and explainable models that say what they saw, what they were not allowed to see, and where they fail. That thread runs through the thesis, the surveys, wearable and physiological signals, and agentic systems that retrieve evidence before they make a claim, including agentic AI for physics-based problems.

The same practice shows up in software. I build production-ready web applications and APIs, from MERN platforms to FastAPI services, so a data pipeline can reach an interface someone actually uses. A research role in deep learning and a software role in web development rest on that shared foundation.
